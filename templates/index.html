📖 Our Story

Where Vietnam Meets Chinese Comfort

For over two decades, Mi La-Cai Noodle House has been a cherished destination on State Street, bringing together the bright, herbaceous flavors of Vietnamese cuisine and the rich, satisfying warmth of Chinese comfort food — all under one roof.

Every bowl of pho is simmered for hours. Every egg roll is hand-rolled with care. Every dish reflects our commitment to authentic recipes, fresh ingredients, and the kind of hospitality that's earned us nearly 1,900 glowing reviews.
