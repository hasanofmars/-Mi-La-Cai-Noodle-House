Our Story

Where Vietnam Meets
Chinese Comfort

For over two decades, Mi La-Cai has been a cherished destination on State Street — bringing together Vietnam's bright, herbaceous flavors and the rich, satisfying warmth of Chinese comfort food.

Every bowl of pho is simmered for hours. Every egg roll is hand-rolled. Every dish reflects our commitment to authentic recipes, fresh ingredients, and genuine hospitality.
