Our Story

Where Vietnam Meets
Chinese Comfort

For over two decades, Mi La-Cai Noodle House has been a cherished destination on State Street, bringing together the bright, herbaceous flavors of Vietnamese cuisine and the rich, satisfying warmth of Chinese comfort food.

Every bowl of pho is simmered for hours. Every egg roll is hand-rolled. Every dish reflects our commitment to authentic recipes, fresh ingredients, and the kind of hospitality that's earned us a loyal following and nearly 1,900 glowing reviews.
